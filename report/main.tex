\begin{abstract}
    This report describes our solution to the Solar Filament Segmentation Challenge~2026 on GONG H-$\alpha$ observations. Our pipeline detects candidates with anchor-free YOLO instance detectors, refines boundaries with a crop-level U-Net, and resolves overlaps into a strictly disjoint instance set under leak-free 3-fold cross-validation and an observation-cluster bootstrap. An EdgeAttNet-inspired~\cite{edgeattnet2025} bottleneck attention refiner initially passed this protocol ($\Delta\mathrm{PQ}{=}{+}0.0028$, 95\% CI $[0.00091,0.00476]$) and moved Public LB from $0.37$ to $0.39$, though a two-seed matched-parameter ablation showed general bottleneck attention capacity --- not the edge prior itself --- drives that gain. Subsequent error decomposition across thirty-plus post-processing and verifier experiments showed the disjoint resolution stage was already a local optimum and detector ranking was the binding constraint. Targeting that bottleneck directly via (i)~mass-preserving inverse anchor-count equalization (\emph{Small-TAL}, $\mathrm{clamp}(10/c,1,4)$) plus training-only stride-8 \emph{spine-auxiliary} supervision on MAGFiLO skeleton polylines, and (ii)~1-to-1 bipartite consensus ($2\text{-of-}3$ at conf~$0.26$) with 3-vote pixelwise refined-crop majority across three diverse full-data detectors (\texttt{YOLO11m-seg}, \texttt{YOLO26m-seg}, and \texttt{YOLO11m-seg} $+$ \emph{Spine} $+$ \emph{Small-TAL}) improved held-out PQ by $+0.0081$ over two folds with \emph{Small-TAL} and $+0.0138$ on fold~2 after adding \emph{Spine-Aux}, lifting Public LB to $\mathbf{0.40}$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
    This report describes our submitted solution to the Solar Filament Segmentation Challenge~2026~\cite{filament-segmentation-2026}, organized by the Earth-Space AI Research (ESAIR) Lab and scored against the MAGFiLO dataset~\cite{ahmadzadeh2024dataset}. Our approach treats the task as instance segmentation: detect each filament as a separate object, refine its boundary, and reconcile overlapping candidates into a single non-overlapping prediction set. Section~\ref{sec:methodology} describes the pipeline; Section~\ref{sec:evaluation} reports our evaluation protocol, results, and an honest account of what we tried that did \emph{not} work.

\subsection{Preprocessing}
    Raw GONG H-$\alpha$ observations are $2048\times2048$ single-channel intensity images. Within the solar disk ($I>\max(4,P_8)$; off-disk pixels zeroed), each image is encoded into 3 channels so the backbone can reuse RGB-pretrained weights on physically meaningful contrast bands: channel~1 clips raw disk intensity to $[P_{0.5},P_{99.5}]$; channel~2 divides by a $\sigma{=}80$\,px Gaussian background estimate (flattening limb darkening), clips to $[P_1,P_{99}]$, and applies CLAHE (clip $2.0$, $16{\times}16$ tiles); channel~3 applies a $31{\times}31$ black-hat transform to channel~2 ($[P_0,P_{99.5}]$) to isolate dark, thin filament detail. (Histogram matching was used only in the rejected pseudo-labeling test.)

\subsection{Detection}
    Baseline candidates come from \textbf{YOLO11m-seg}~\cite{yolo11} at $1280{\times}1280$ (single class, up to 70 epochs, patience 14, batch 4, seed $3407{+}\text{fold}$, \texttt{deterministic=True}; AdamW $\text{lr}_0{=}7{\times}10^{-4}$, $\text{lr}_f{=}0.05$, weight decay $5{\times}10^{-4}$, 3-epoch warmup; rotation $\pm180^\circ$, translate $0.04$, scale $0.25$, flips $p{=}0.5$, mosaic $0.20$ off last 10 epochs, value jitter $0.18$, mask ratio 2; inference conf $0.26$, NMS IoU $0.55$). Same-split RF-DETR-Seg~\cite{rfdetr2025} trailed raw PQ by $-0.057$. To break the single-detector recall/FP frontier (Section~\ref{sec:strengths}), our primary submission pairs full-data \texttt{YOLO11m-seg} with two orthogonal full-data detectors: \textbf{YOLO26m-seg}~\cite{yolo11} (\texttt{ultralytics}~8.4.120) and \textbf{YOLO11m-seg + Spine-Aux + Small-TAL} trained with (1)~mass-preserving anchor equalization in \texttt{v8SegmentationLoss} ($\mathrm{clamp}(10/c,1,4)$ for a target assigned $c$ of top-$10$ anchors, normalizing positive \texttt{cls/box} and \texttt{seg} sums so conf~$0.26$ calibration stays invariant), and (2)~a training-only stride-8 P3 head ($\lambda_{\text{aux}}{=}0.5$, pos/neg-balanced BCE) on rasterized MAGFiLO \texttt{spine} polylines transformed identically under mosaic/perspective via a reserved pseudo-class.

\subsection{Boundary Refinement}\label{sec:refiner}
    Raw detector masks are accurate in extent but imprecise at the pixel boundary, which is exactly where the official metric is least forgiving (Section~\ref{sec:evaluation-strategy}). We refine each candidate independently: a square crop is taken around the candidate ($1.65\times$ its bounding box, minimum side 96px, $320\times320$px after resizing), passed through a U-Net refiner, and the resulting probability map is thresholded (0.45) and intersected with the original candidate mask (\emph{shrink} refinement — the refiner may only remove pixels, not add them outside the detector's proposal).

    \textbf{Training data.} Positive crops are generated one per ground-truth instance per annotator (a 3-annotator image contributes up to $3\times$ the crops of a single-annotator image), centered and scaled from that instance's box with the same $1.65\times$-context rule as inference; no hard-negative crops. Augmentation: random $90^\circ$ rotations, flips, mild gamma jitter on the first two channels.

    \textbf{Optimization.} We minimize $0.35\cdot\text{BCE} + 0.65\cdot\text{soft-Dice} + 0.15\cdot\text{boundary-}L_1$, where the boundary term compares a morphological-gradient (dilation$-$erosion, kernel 5) map of prediction and target. AdamW (weight decay $10^{-4}$), lr $2{\times}10^{-4}$ cosine-annealed, AMP, batch size 8. Per-fold refiners train 14 epochs (seed 3407), keeping the best validation crop-Dice checkpoint. The submitted refiner is fine-tuned from the fold-0 checkpoint on all 707 images for 5 epochs at $0.35\times$ the base lr (Section~\ref{sec:cv}); with no held-out data, we keep the final-epoch weights.

    Our refiner, \texttt{MediSegEdgeAttUNet}, extends this U-Net~\cite{ronneberger2015unet} with a single \textbf{edge-guided multi-head self-attention} (EG-MHSA) block at the bottleneck (8 heads), \emph{inspired by} EdgeAttNet~\cite{edgeattnet2025}: a $3{\times}3$ conv on the crop yields an edge prior, projected and added to the attention query and key (not value), before residual and layer norm. EdgeAttNet's public code lacks a usable implementation of this module, so we could not validate against it, and our design deviates: EdgeAttNet stacks \emph{two} 4-head blocks and \emph{drops} positional encodings, shrinking to 22.7M params (vs.\ its 31.0M baseline), while ours \emph{adds} 4.2M (35.2M vs.\ 31.0M) --- matching the parameter count of EdgeAttNet's own ``U-Net + MHSA, no positional encoding'' ablation almost exactly. We therefore call it edge-attention-inspired, not a reproduction; Section~\ref{sec:evaluation} revisits what this coincidence implies.

\subsection{Instance Resolution}
    Refined candidates can still overlap. In the multi-detector setting, proposals ($\ge\!20$\,px at conf $\ge\!0.08$) are first grouped across detectors via greedy descending-IoU \textbf{1-to-1 bipartite matching} ($\text{IoU}\ge 0.45$, at most one proposal per detector per cluster, preventing transitive chaining across adjacent filaments); clusters with $\ge\!2$ detectors above $0.26$ are kept, and 3-member clusters fuse their refined crops by pixelwise majority (\texttt{majority\_3plus}, falling back to highest-confidence member on 2-way matches so thin tips are not clipped). Across surviving instances, we drop candidates $\ge\!90\%$ contained in a larger one and greedily assign pixels in ascending area order ($\ge\!20$\,px kept), which beat confidence-first and area-descending orderings by $0.012$--$0.015$~PQ across all three folds.

\subsection{Cross-Validation and Final Model}\label{sec:cv}
    The competition provides 707 training observations, each annotated by one or more experts (8{,}199 instance annotations across 1{,}154 image--annotator records; all annotators used as independent targets). We group by unique observation filename --- preventing fold-boundary leakage by construction --- and partition into three folds (236/236/235) with a stratified $k$-fold splitter (seed 3407) on quintiles of each image's reference-annotator instance count. One detector and one refiner are trained per fold during model selection. Keeping cross-validated architecture and threshold choices frozen, the final submission retrains the three selected detectors on \emph{all} 707 observations (1{,}154 records, up to 70 epochs after a $+0.0099$ data-scaling gate; Ultralytics \texttt{best.pt} monitored on an in-sample fold) and fine-tunes the refiner on all 707 observations (warm-started from fold~0, 5 epochs, $0.35\times$ base lr, final-epoch weights).

\section{Evaluation}\label{sec:evaluation}

\subsection{Evaluation Strategy}\label{sec:evaluation-strategy}
    The competition's official metric is Panoptic Quality (PQ), following the definition of Kirillov et al.~\cite{kirillov2019panoptic} but scored by the competition's own reference implementation~\cite{selfeval2026}, which we replicated locally against that notebook's test cases. Unlike the original PQ formulation's one-to-one Hungarian matching, the competition scorer counts \emph{every} prediction-ground-truth pair with IoU above $0.5$ as a true positive (i.e.\ both sides can be matched multiply), so
    \[
        \mathrm{PQ} = \frac{\sum_{(p,g)\in TP}\mathrm{IoU}(p,g)}{|TP| + 0.5|FP| + 0.5|FN|}.
    \]
    A prediction with IoU just under 0.5 scores zero \emph{and} incurs a false-positive/false-negative penalty, so near-miss boundary quality dominates the score far more than raw detection recall. Matching is done per (annotator, image) pair and micro-averaged, not averaged per-image.

    Because annotators disagree, we measured a reference-\hspace{0pt}annotation agreement baseline: scoring one expert's annotations as the ``prediction'' against every other expert on the same image, under the identical official rule, gives PQ$=0.3398$ (multi-annotator images only, $n{=}296$; an earlier version trivially included self-matches, inflating it to $0.76$ --- corrected). Not a hard ceiling (ours scores above it); it is a benchmark for labeling-task reproducibility.

    Baseline, refiner, and post-processing choices use the leak-free 3-fold cross-\hspace{0pt}validation of Section~\ref{sec:cv}. To avoid a failure mode we caught twice --- a change that improves one fold but does not replicate --- we compute an observation-cluster bootstrap~\cite{efron1993bootstrap} (2000 resamples, PQ as ratio-of-sums) requiring fold-sign agreement and a pooled 95\% CI above zero ($2\times 0.5^3 = \allowbreak 0.25$ for sign alone); compute-heavy late-stage 3-detector consensus was validated on two folds ($n{=}471$) for \emph{Small-TAL} and held-out fold~2 ($n{=}235$) plus a predeclared Public LB~${>}0.39$ gate for \emph{+Spine-Aux}. Pooled intervals are reported for accepted changes (rejected in Section~\ref{sec:strengths}), with no multiple-testing adjustment across the thirty-plus experiments evaluated on these splits.

\subsection{Strengths and Weaknesses}\label{sec:strengths}
    \textbf{Strengths.} Five candidates were rejected under independent fold confirmation, bootstrap intervals, or matched LB checks (mask fusion, threshold retuning, refiner retraining, pseudo-labeling, 3-fold ensembling). The first accepted change --- edge-guided attention --- did not survive the matched-parameter, two-seed check (Section~\ref{sec:results-discussion}), proving fold-sign agreement plus a bootstrap CI cannot separate general capacity from a specific mechanism without a matched control.

    \textbf{Weaknesses.} Hard misses (2{,}138 FN, 1{,}561 FP) dominate single-detector output; Figure~\ref{fig:example}(b) shows an instance-separation failure boundary refinement cannot fix. Fold-2 error decomposition (Figure~\ref{fig:anatomy}) attributes ${\sim}65\%$ of FNs to cases with no proposal at $0.26$ (median ${\sim}715$\,px, mostly single-annotator objects), yet ${\sim}57\%$ have an IoU~$>0.5$ candidate below it. Over two dozen mechanisms failed the frozen-point gate ($\Delta\mathrm{PQ}<+0.005$) or fold replication across six families: \emph{training tricks} (EMA, multi-channel refiner, copy-paste, dense oversampling, DT loss); \emph{extra proposal sources} (sub-threshold rescue; RF-DETR union, $-0.028$; semantic U-Net union, $-0.031$; $2{\times}2$ tiling, $-0.033$); \emph{label structure} (soft/union labels); \emph{auxiliary signals} (temporal neighbors, magnetograms); \emph{verifiers} (CNN verifier AUC $0.64<$ conf $0.80$); and \emph{SimCLR pretraining} ($-0.004$). A Cellpose-style flow separator ($-0.021$) and a weak/strong consensus-class head also lost. Expert pairs agree at matched IoU $0.634$ ($56\%$ cross-annotator recall; ${\sim}44\%$ single-annotator calls), yet under per-(annotator, image) scoring ($ks>\mathrm{PQ}\cdot R/2$) human-level detection would reach PQ ${\approx}0.53$--$0.55$, so the binding limit is detector ranking rather than label ceiling.

    A terminal 3-fold audit ($0.446\pm0.001$) showed ${\sim}24\%$ of emitted instances have negative marginal value, yet a 19-feature emission gate lost $0.008$--$0.029$ per fold, and overlapping-mask probes ($+0.013$/$+0.06$ locally) were rejected at ingestion because the host scores only strictly disjoint sets --- closing the post-processing space. An initial consensus run (YOLO11m, $3{\times}3$ crop-tile, YOLO26m) had to retract its $+0.0116$ fold-2 reading after catching eval-fold leakage in the crop detector; rebuilt leak-free, it gave $+0.0030$ (fold~0) and $+0.0086$ (fold~2), scoring $0.39$ publicly. Replacing transitive clustering and the weak crop detector with 1-to-1 bipartite matching, \texttt{majority\_3plus} crop voting, \emph{Small-TAL} ($+0.0081$ 2-fold PQ, $n{=}471$, 95\% CI $[0.0030,0.0130]$, $P(\Delta{>}0){=}0.9995$), and training-only \emph{Spine-Aux} ($\lambda_{\text{aux}}{=}0.5$, 100\% valid spines across all 8{,}199 annotations) raised fold-2 OOF to $0.4580$ (Table~\ref{tab:breakthrough}, Figure~\ref{fig:breakthrough}; $\Delta{=}{+}0.0138$, FP $-15.7\%$, $+15$ TP; $77\%$-GT-subsampled sparse-label proxy $0.4028$ vs.\ $0.3896$) and cleared our predeclared $>0.39$ gate on Public LB ($\mathbf{0.40}$). \textbf{Future work:} multi-seed temporal-block validation and joint detector--refiner training.
